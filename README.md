# Hyderabad Tycoon 🏙️

A cute city-building tycoon game set in Hyderabad. Lay roads, grow neighbourhoods from the Old City to
HITEC City, and unlock landmarks like Charminar as your city grows.

**▶ Play it: https://avinash-kadabur.github.io/hyderabad-tycoon/**

It works on phones and computers. Android and iPhone apps will follow.

## How to play (so far)

- Pick a tool at the bottom: Road, Homes, Shops, IT Offices or Bulldoze.
- Tap a tile to build, or drag to build a whole line.
- To look around, drag with **Move**, use two fingers, or right-click and drag. Pinch or scroll to zoom.
- You can't build on water, and Deccan boulders have to be bulldozed first.

Keyboard: `1` to `6` pick tools, and `Esc` goes back to Move.
